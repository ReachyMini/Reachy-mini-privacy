Reachy Mini AI

Privacy Policy — WHOOP Wearable Integration

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Academic Research Prototype

Reachy Mini AI is an experimental academic research project exploring socially assistive robotics, artificial intelligence, and wearable technology. It is not a medical device.

1. Introduction

Reachy Mini AI is an academic research prototype designed to investigate how artificial intelligence and wearable physiological measurements may support accessible, sensory-friendly human-robot interactions.

This policy describes the intended processing of WHOOP wearable information within the research prototype.

2. Information We Access

With appropriate authorization, the application may access the following WHOOP measurements:

  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Sleep duration and sleep information
  • Recovery scores
  • Daily physiological activity summaries

The application is designed to request only information necessary for its research functionality.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

WHOOP measurements may be used to:

  • Provide optional wellbeing check-ins.
  • Support personalized, sensory-friendly interactions.
  • Explore physiological data integration with Agentic AI.
  • Evaluate the technical performance of the prototype.

Physiological measurements are not used to diagnose autism, determine emotional states, or provide medical treatment.

4. Data Storage

The intended implementation processes WHOOP measurements locally on the research computer.

Raw physiological measurements are not intended to be permanently stored. Temporary measurements may remain in computer memory while the application is running.

These data-handling practices must be verified before processing real participant information.

5. Data Sharing

The application is designed to process raw wearable measurements locally rather than transmit them to external AI services.

WHOOP independently processes wearable information according to its own privacy practices.

The research prototype does not intentionally sell wearable information or use it for advertising.

6. Consent and Authorization

Access to WHOOP information requires authorization through WHOOP's OAuth 2.0 authentication process.

Users may decline wearable integration and may withdraw authorization through the applicable WHOOP account controls.

7. Children's Privacy

The project explores socially assistive robotics, including potential applications involving autistic children.

Research involving children will require appropriate guardian consent, institutional approval where applicable, and additional privacy safeguards.

The prototype is not currently authorized for unsupervised collection of children's physiological information.

8. Data Security

The system is designed to use authenticated API connections and local data processing.

API credentials and authorization tokens must be protected against unauthorized access.

Security measures will be reviewed before testing with real research participants.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

The intended design does not permanently retain raw physiological measurements.

Temporary measurements are intended to be cleared when the application session ends.

Users may withdraw authorization to prevent future access to their WHOOP information.
